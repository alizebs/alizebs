A Beautiful Face

Overview


Skip Overview

This story is a short psychological horror about beauty, material obsession, and revenge.
The protagonist was left by her lover for another woman and was humiliated for her looks. She has been living alone since their seperation, the last three months. Consumed by long resentment, she plans to take revenge. Her plan grows into something far more sinister as her angst and anger takes form of witchcraft.

A Beautiful Face

A girl with messed hair and clothes went inside her house. The shorter hand of the clock turned to eleven, merely visible in the little light of her house. She likes to live in the dark, alone, with her dog, who looks as dead as her. She pulled out from her bag — picture of herself and a man; his arm on her shoulder, both smiling happily.
She mercilessly tore the picture into two, taking his picture apart. Soaked it in a bowl of red water, then covered it with clayey mud and wrapped it in a small black cloth and tied it with threads so as to form a doll. The red water was poured in meat and served to her dog. She pet the dog while it was eating, looking at it like a psychopath. "I don't look good, dear?" she said it to herself, loud. She remembered his words, "Your face is like a literal dog."
"Your face is like a dog, isn't it, my dear. does that mean you are not beautiful?," she said to her dog, staring at it with wide open eyes, turning and twisting her head, "No, my dear, you're absolutely gorgeous."

"She looks good, doesn't she? You like her for her looks, don't you?" she murmured to herself as she placed the doll in a completely dark room beneath the staircase, in the middle of a salt drawn ring hovered on a six legged star. She then placed candles on each end and lit them. Her dog started barking and screaming, still outside the room, as she threw water with her fingers, blown on with words from a book that looked as it was recovered from an ancient underground treasure. Moments later, the candles began shaking — harder and harder — until the flame kissed the salt.
He was sitting in his couch. It was midnight — his working time for the night shift. The 'good looking lady' served for him a cup of coffee and went back to the kitchen.
As all the candles fell and blew off, she kissed the doll on the forehead and started petting it as if it was alive.
"She is so beautiful isn't she? You like looking at her don't you?" she started laughing out like crazy, with her shrill voice, louder, louder — and louder.
"Look at her!", she screamed; "Look at her! Look at her! Look at her!"
"Look at her, dear, take your chance!"
She took a pin and looked at it, twisting her neck to her left shoulder — then the right. "Look at her!" she screamed as she aggressively stabbed it on the top left of the head of the doll.
The man took the last sip of his coffee and put it down on the saucer on the table right in front of him. He started feeling irritation on his left eye. It began to itch and slowly turned red. He twitched his cheek to his eye and picked his hand up to search for what was wrong. As his middle finger reached the eyebag then towards the eyeball — but before he could touch it, it exploded with blood. The blood flew over his cheek, his clothes, the couch and the table to the wall straight. He covered his eye with his hand — screaming in immense pain and anguish. Upon hearing this, his new and more beautiful woman came to him. "How did this happen?!" she screamed as she saw the horrible sight of gore.

This was not it, she picked another pin and stabbed it on the top-right of the doll. She screamed, "Seeing is a beautiful phenomenon itself, but you, you skin scavenger — you don't deserve it!"

As his second eye began turning red, he screamed, "No, no, no, no — no!"
His right eye exploded, just like the other — smearing blood all over her hand, her clothes and her face — beautiful face. She started screaming for help. Dialed her phone, "Someone, Help!!" she cried.
He was admitted to the hospital, but shortly after, he died due to blood loss and brain damage. The doctor declared, "This cannot be an internal injury, it is verily a physical attack." Where he was alone with his wife, when there is no witness — alive — to say the truth; who will be blamed for the fatal sin — the one present with him, inside his house for the last three months; or the one who lives far away, and has never visited him since they seperated three months ago.

She cried, shouted, screamed to say her truth; but who believes the accused - who has no witness.
Her lips were numb, her eyes were still, her hands were trembling she stood straight on the trapdoor — and the rope around her neck waited to be tightened. The executioner looked at his watch and waved his hand. Seeing the black cloth being brought to her, she tried to scream and cry — but her throat refused — her mouth lay open and her jaw shriveled as they covered with it — her beautiful face.
