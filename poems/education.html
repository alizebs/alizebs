The Place of Education

I can burn your canvas, shut your voice,
I can hide your screens, silence your guitar,
I can demolish your stove, destroy your oven,
I can stab your heart — your love, your feels;

There exists no desire I can't kill
Your creativity will wither, slow and still.

Hold your thirsts, forget your hunger;
Bear faintness, break your slumber;
For money, for future, for your ambition.
I am what the call — the place of education.

—alizebs
