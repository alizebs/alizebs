Of Enemies and Beloveds

The enemy across the blood-soaked gravels;
sons of witches, daughters of devils.
I dream of the day we've killed them all.
The word came: they've crossed the wall;

Faces of humans — must've hid their horns.
One demon screamed till his jaws were torn:
"This, soldiers, is the hell the enemies wait in;
Sons of Lucifer, daughters of Satan.
Unseathe your swords and kill them all,
for your beloveds behind the wall.

—alizebs
