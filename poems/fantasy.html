Fantasy de Thee and Me

When you dive into your fantasy,
What do you see, what do you see?

I see a world that’s fashioned old,
A world with black suits & red frocks;

Gardens of the reddest rose—
And flying therein the blackest crows;

That castles with the heavenly doors,
That castles with the gold indoors;

Kingdoms, kings & emperors,
Queens, prince, princess & shehzadors;

That streets with the candle-lantern glow
Wandering through them on a horse

Shooting arrows — aiming on the bow,
the shield protects, attacks the sword;

A pegasis flies as its wings blows,
Riding it over the clouds’ glow;

Was it or will it be true — who knows
Fantasy de thee & me are a vast whole world.

—alizebs

MMXXIII
